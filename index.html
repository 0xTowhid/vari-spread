Variational spreads & open interest

Buy, sell, spread and OI for every listed market.

Buy = ask, Sell = bid for the chosen size. "Spread as in app" is half the bid–ask gap over mid (0.033% on 787.938 / 787.412); "Full bid–ask" is double. Quotes can be cached up to 10 minutes. $1M quotes exist for majors only. OI is shown in USD. The API has no group, type or sector fields, so these mirror the app by rule: TradFi markets are treated as SWAP and crypto as PERP, and sectors come from ticker lists. Edit OVERRIDES in the script to fix any asset. Rate limit: 10 requests per 10 seconds.
